FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast can you start?

Scoping call within 48 hours of your message, a fixed scope proposal in the same 48 hours, and kickoff typically inside a week.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do, 100%. Repositories, infrastructure and documentation are transferred to you on completion. No vendor lock in, ever.

How do you price projects?

Two models: fixed scope packages with a fixed quote, or a dedicated squad billed monthly. You always know the number before we start.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a free audit, stabilize what exists, then ship. We regularly rescue stalled or legacy projects.

What happens after launch?

Support plans backed by SLAs cover monitoring, bug fixes, security patches and a monthly improvement budget.
